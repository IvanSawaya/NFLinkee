How to Play NFL Linkee

Four questions. One hidden connection. Find the link, win the point.

  1. Split into teams (2–6 works best).
  2. A card appears with four questions. The reader reads them out one at a time.
  3. Teams can shout out answers, but the real prize is the LINK — the one thing all four answers have in common.
  4. Tap a tile to reveal its answer. Tap Reveal the Link when a team thinks they've cracked it.
  5. Award points with the + buttons on the scoreboard. (House rule: 1 point for the link, −1 for a wrong guess.)
  6. Hit Next Card and repeat. Highest score when the deck runs out wins.

Keyboard shortcuts

1 2 3 4 reveal that answer  ·  Space reveal the link  ·  N next card
